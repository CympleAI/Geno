# Geno
An open and free future personal assistant system

## What is needed to build a personal assistant?

- Network and Computing Resources
  - A communication network that connects all your devices.
  - Efficient utilization of your hardware resources, enabling interaction between lightweight devices and computing devices.
  - Two modes of connectivity: cloud-based services and self-hosted local services.

- Lightweight Relay Services
  - Collection and forwarding of communication/chat data
  - Collection and forwarding of emails
  - Collection and forwarding of articles/news
  - Collection and forwarding of various other types of information

- Heavyweight Computing Services
  - A Large Language Model–based central brain (can be replaced with OpenAI, Gemini, etc.)
  - Speech, image, and video recognition services
  - Speech, image, and video generation/output services
  - Data analysis services
  - AI agent framework with self-upgrade capability
